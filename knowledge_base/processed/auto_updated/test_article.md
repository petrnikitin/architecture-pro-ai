# Test Article: The Nexus Protocol

## Overview
The Nexus Protocol is a revolutionary communication system used throughout the Cyber Nexus universe. It allows instantaneous data transfer across vast distances using quantum entanglement principles.

## Key Features
- **Instantaneous Communication**: Messages are transmitted faster than light
- **Secure Encryption**: Uses 2048-bit quantum keys
- **Redundant Nodes**: Multiple relay stations ensure reliability
- **Universal Compatibility**: Works with all species' technology

## History
The Nexus Protocol was developed by the Synthari Order during the Great Expansion. It replaced older, slower systems and unified the galaxy's communication infrastructure.

## Technical Specifications
- Range: Up to 50,000 light-years
- Bandwidth: 10 petabytes per second
- Latency: < 1 millisecond
- Error rate: 0.0001%

## Usage
To connect to the Nexus Protocol, one must possess a certified Nexus Communicator device. These devices are regulated by the Galactic Council to prevent misuse.
